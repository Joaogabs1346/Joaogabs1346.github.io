Who is João Gabriel



Hi! I'm João Gabriel, a Senior GenAI Software Engineer at Deloitte and an AI Researcher at CEIA. I design and ship production AI systems built on Large Language Models: multi-agent architectures, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and agent orchestration with the Model Context Protocol (MCP), running on Kubernetes and serverless cloud infrastructure.

My background is in NLP research. I led the training of MED-LLM-BR, the first clinical LLM for Brazilian Portuguese, which won the 3rd Place Best Paper Award at BRACIS 2024. Today I focus on turning LLMs into reliable products that solve real business problems, from fine-tuning and evaluation to deployment at scale.
